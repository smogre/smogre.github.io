Background

Before joining the Allen Institute, I completed my Ph.D. in Physics at the University of California, San Diego, where I worked with Prof. Elena Koslover as a Virtual Molecular Cell Consortium (VMCC) Fellow on theoretical and computational models of intracellular transport and organelle dynamics.

I hold a Dual Degree (B.Tech. & M.Tech.) in Engineering Physics with a Minor in Biosciences & Bioengineering from the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay.
